# Weekly Primary Bet Verification

An authenticated browser session saved a real Customer Evidence decision from the Founder Operating Desk, then opened the Weekly Review agenda. The decision appeared as an unresolved agenda item with review, accountability, and primary-bet actions.

The primary-bet workflow persists one selected record for the current Monday-starting week and exposes its next action in the Founder Operating Desk. Completion keeps the selected weekly plan visible without modifying the original decision evidence or outcome. TypeScript and the production build passed after implementation.
