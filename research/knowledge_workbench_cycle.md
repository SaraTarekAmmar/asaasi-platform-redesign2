# Knowledge Workbench and Evidence Recovery Research

**Research date:** 22 August 2026  
**Scope:** Turning retained customer evidence into practical founder guidance without detaching claims from sources.

## Browser-verified competitor findings

Productboard’s current insights guidance recommends grouping related feedback in focused boards, retaining the relationship between feedback and the relevant feature idea, and revisiting broader customer input rather than optimizing only for the initiative already in progress.[1]

Aha!’s current discovery guidance frames a customer database as the foundation for consistent user research. Its discovery product taxonomy foregrounds interviews, customer feedback, product knowledge, and research context as connected product-development inputs rather than isolated notes.[2]

Dovetail’s current customer-intelligence experience reinforces an additional guardrail: reusable answers and generated summaries should remain grounded in customer evidence, with source context available rather than a black-box conclusion.[3]

## ASaaSI implication

ASaaSI Knowledge should be a **field guide built from retained work**, not a generic article directory. The most valuable next step is a founder-owned claim record that preserves the source decision or customer evidence, identifies the context in which the guidance applies, names the current operating rule, and points to a fresh evidence move. This must remain explicitly revisable and never convert repeated language into a fabricated universal truth.

## ASaaSI audit and selected scope

The former Knowledge route was a static recommended-path and content listing. It did not recover retained workflow records, distinguish evidence from advice, or give a founder a way to retain an explicitly revisable operating rule. It was therefore disconnected from the evidence-to-action system used by Customer Evidence, Activity, and Weekly Review.

The rebuilt **Knowledge Field Guide** selects one eligible saved decision, keeps the source title, retained excerpt, original test, and review point visible, and requires the founder to write a current working rule, a defined scope, and one next evidence move that could revise it. The guide persists on the original workflow record and can be reopened from a retained archive. It does not generate advice, rank source records, merge evidence, imply an outcome, or replace the Founder Learning Archive.

## Validation note

TypeScript and production builds pass. The available browser session was unauthenticated during route inspection, so the implementation was validated through the typed, compiled workflow rather than by introducing synthetic founder records only to populate the guide.

Desktop and 375px Arabic RTL protected-route views were also checked after refining the access objects. Knowledge now exposes Claim → Evidence → Test → Return and Activity registrations expose Date → Room → Follow-up → Return before credential entry. The review request to add circular nodes or rings was not adopted because ASaaSI explicitly prohibits circular and ring decoration; active rectangular markers, sequenced numerals, and narrow saffron rules carry state instead.
