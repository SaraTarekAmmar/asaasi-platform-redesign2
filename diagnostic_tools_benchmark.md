# Remaining Diagnostic Workbench Benchmark

## Research purpose

This continuation reviews public diagnostic patterns for **customer health**, **pricing**, and **cash runway**. The purpose is to borrow useful interaction and decision-model patterns without reproducing a competitor’s visual identity, claims, benchmarks, output language, or product architecture.

## Patterns reviewed

| Source | Public pattern observed | Useful principle | ASaaSI adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Gainsight customer health score guide](https://www.gainsight.com/blog/customer-health-scores/) | Combines product usage, support history, sentiment, and engagement; suggests explicit metric weighting and segment-aware interpretation. | A health record should show the signal basis and attach an intervention, rather than present a mysterious score. | Build a **Signal → Friction → Intervention** record. Founders state the observed signal, customer cohort, confidence, and one bounded recovery test. Avoid false precision and generic traffic-light scoring. |
| [PlanMySaaS pricing calculator](https://www.planmysaas.com/tools/saas-pricing-calculator) | Collects cost, target margin, market range, customer base, and growth; returns pricing tiers and projections. | Pricing work needs cost context, market evidence, and an explicit test design. | Build a **Value → Evidence → Test** pricing record. Ask for the buyer job, alternative, value proof, cost floor, and reversible pricing experiment. Do not display market benchmarks or generated price promises. |
| [StartupCFO runway calculator](https://www.startupcfo.ai/tools/runway) | Begins with cash balance, monthly expenses, and monthly revenue; distinguishes gross and net burn and links runway to planning windows. | Runway decisions should identify cash inputs and the next operational action. | Build a **Cash → Assumption → Decision** runway record. Founders identify cash available, monthly committed outflow, reliable inflow, and the next proof or cost decision. Label all planning values as illustrative and avoid investment advice. |

## Product decisions

ASaaSI will preserve the landing-derived editorial visual system: off-white canvas, navy type, fine grey rules, saffron only for active nodes and provenance markers, Manrope headings, DM Sans interface text, and evidence-led route objects. These workbenches will not imitate dark calculator panels, generic KPI dashboards, or competitor-specific score bands.

Each tool will expose a functional working object in the first viewport and conclude with one **bounded experiment record**, not a verdict, benchmark score, funding recommendation, or automated financial advice.
