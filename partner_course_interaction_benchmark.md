# Partner, Course, and Interaction Benchmark

## Public patterns reviewed

| Source | Useful public pattern | ASaaSI adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Make partner page | Separates partnership types by the job they enable, then provides an explicit route for joining, enabling, and finding partners. | Use a clear partner ecosystem with **what a partner contributes**, **which founder outcome it supports**, and two unambiguous paths: browse partners or apply to collaborate. |
| PartnerStack directory | Puts search, category filtering, and fit comparison ahead of large program cards, while keeping a distinct path to program listing and partner sign-in. | Use a searchable, stage-aware partner register with light category filters, evidence fields, and a separate **Apply to become a partner** path. Never use claimed payouts, discounts, or unverified benefits. |

## Design decisions for ASaaSI

ASaaSI will not reproduce partner-program pricing, revenue-share claims, logos, or interface styling. Its partner experience should operate as a calm editorial register: **Partner → Founder job → Available path**. Each entry names the service category, the founder decision it supports, eligibility or stage context, and the next handoff. The application path should collect a service proposition, evidence of practice, regional context, and the expected founder outcome before a review state is shown.

For courses and the seven-stage roadmap, the same principle applies: the visible object should be the work of the stage, not a generic enrollment card. A stage journey must show the decision, evidence, and bounded practice that a founder leaves with. Motion should guide attention between this sequence and respect reduced-motion preferences; it should never obscure a choice or delay completion of a form.
