## Approved Brand Foundation

> **ASaaSI is the operating system for SaaS founders in the Middle East — the people, knowledge, and AI they need to build, launch, and grow, in one place.**

The claim must always be unpacked: **Education, Community, and Tools** are the three visible pillars; the **SaaS AI Coach** is the connected layer across them, not a fourth standalone pillar. The permanent tagline is **“SaaS, Powered By Everything That Matters.”**

Every page should be direct, regional, stage-specific, evidence-led, and encouraging without hype. It should not call generic startup content, non-localised Western playbooks, future technology, or unvalidated traction a finished ASaaSI capability.

## Revenue Architecture and Presentation Rule

The model has five streams: **annual Subscriber access, cohort courses, workshops, sponsorships, and capped service-provider listings.** The visitor-to-revenue flow is:

> Visitor → Registered (free profile) → Subscriber (paid annual access) → paid course buyer / workshop attendee, with provider and sponsor pathways running in parallel as B2B tracks.

The supplied three-year model calculates **$95,460 / $323,800 / $998,000** in Years 1–3 from assumptions for all five streams. These amounts and all price inputs are **illustrative planning placeholders**, not confirmed pricing, public forecasts, investor guidance, or promises. Any website presentation must show stream mechanics and editable-planning assumptions; if totals are shown, it must visibly carry this exact caveat.

These are founder-company flows, distinct from ASaaSI’s internal functional map above.
